My pick: an AI assistant for freelancers

Working name: FreelanceFlow

Why this one: The pain is clear (freelancers lose hours on proposals, invoices, and chasing payments), they already pay for tools, and the core features can be built cheaply with an AI API. Subscriptions fit naturally, and you can launch without payment-processing or compliance headaches.

The MVP

Target user: Solo freelancers in one niche, like designers, writers, or developers. Choose one first so your templates and messaging feel tailored.

Core promise: "Go from client inquiry to paid invoice without the admin work."

Only three features:

Proposal generator. The user pastes a client's message or job post and answers 3-4 quick questions (scope, price, timeline). The AI drafts a professional proposal they can edit and export as PDF.
Simple invoicing. Create an invoice from a proposal in one click. Include a client list, line items, due date, and a shareable link or PDF. Payment is marked manually at first.
Smart payment reminders. Track unpaid invoices and draft polite follow-up messages at set intervals (3 days before due, on due date, 7 days late). The user approves and sends.

Leave out for now: time tracking, contracts, built-in payments, team accounts, analytics, a native mobile app. Add these only if users ask repeatedly.

Suggested tech stack (cheap and fast)
Frontend: Next.js or a no-code builder like FlutterFlow/Bubble if you don't code
Backend and database: Supabase or Firebase (auth, database, storage)
AI: Claude API for proposal and reminder drafting
PDF export: a library like React-PDF or a template service
Email sending: Resend or Postmark
Pricing
Free: 3 proposals and 3 invoices per month
Pro: $8-12/month for unlimited use, custom branding, and automatic reminders
4-week build plan
Week 1: Talk to 10-15 freelancers in your chosen niche. Ask how they write proposals and chase payments today. Build a clickable mockup.
Week 2: Build the proposal generator and test it yourself.
Week 3: Add invoicing and reminders.
Week 4: Launch to 20-30 beta users from freelancer communities (Reddit, Facebook groups, X, LinkedIn, Discord) and watch how they use it.
How to know it's working
At least 40% of signups create a proposal in their first session
Users come back to send a second or third invoice
5-10 beta users say they'd pay, and ideally actually do
